**The Python in the Jungle**  

In the heart of the dense jungle, a wise old snake named **P7** slithered through the vines. P7 wasn't just any snake-he was a **Python**, the master of logic, loops, and elegant syntax.  

One day, P7 discovered a hidden cave filled with mysterious symbols: `A, B, C, D...Y, Z, 0, 1, 2, 3...8, 9`. "Ah, the building blocks of the universe!" he hissed. With a flick of his tail, he rearranged the symbols into perfect sequences:  
```python
for x in range(4):
    print(chr(65 + x), x)
```  
A parrot, **Polly**, watched in awe. "Squawk! What magic is this?"  

"It's code," P7 grinned. "A language that speaks to the world."  

From that day on, the jungle thrived on Pythonic wisdom, where every creature learned the art of loops, conditions, and recursion. The trees whispered algorithms, and the rivers flowed in **beautifully indented** patterns.  

And so, in the jungle where logic ruled, **Python** became more than just a snake-it became **a way of thinking**.
